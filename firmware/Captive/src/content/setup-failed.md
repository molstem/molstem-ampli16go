## Connection Failed

Your device could not establish a network connection. This may be due to:

- Incorrect WiFi password
- Network out of range or unavailable
- Router configuration blocking new devices
- Temporary network issues

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. **Verify credentials** — Double-check your WiFi password
2. **Check signal strength** — Move closer to your WiFi router
3. **Router settings** — Ensure your router allows new device connections
4. **Try another network** — Use a different WiFi network if available
5. **Restart router** — Power cycle your WiFi router and try again

### Still Having Issues?

If problems persist, you can:
- Connect via USB for initial setup
- Contact support at [molstem.com/support](https://molstem.com/support)
