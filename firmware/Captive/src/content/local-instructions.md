## Your Device is Ready

You can now access your molstem thermocycler from any browser on your local network.

### Quick Access

Use either link above to open the device interface:
- The `.local` address uses mDNS and works on most networks
- If mDNS doesn't resolve, use the IP address directly

### Getting Started

1. Open the device interface in your browser
2. Log in with the password you created
3. Load or create a PCR protocol
4. Insert your samples and begin your experiment

### Need Help?

For documentation, protocols, and support visit [molstem.com/docs](https://molstem.com/docs)
