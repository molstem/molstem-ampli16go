## Complete Cloud Registration

Your device is configured for cloud operation. Complete registration to enable remote access and cloud features.

### Register Your Device

1. Visit [app.molstem.com/devices](https://app.molstem.com/devices)
2. Sign in or create a free account
3. Click **Add Device** in your dashboard
4. Enter the enrollment ID shown above

### Cloud Features

- **Remote Access** — Monitor and control experiments from anywhere
- **Protocol Library** — Store and sync your PCR protocols across devices
- **Experiment History** — View temperature graphs and logs from past runs
- **Fleet Management** — Manage multiple devices from one dashboard
- **Collaboration** — Share protocols with your team

Your device will appear in your dashboard within minutes of registration.
